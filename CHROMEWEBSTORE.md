## Detailed Description
Drunken Semaphore transforms your new tab into an aesthetic, focused, and relaxing handheld console sanctuary. Whether you are studying, coding, reading, or chilling, Drunken Semaphore provides everything you need in a unified, retro-futuristic glassmorphism workspace.

### Key Features:
- **Customizable Clock & World Clock:** Minimalist time display, date, and multiple timezone trackers (Istanbul, New York, London, Tokyo).
- **Pomodoro Focus Timer:** Pre-configured intervals (25/5, 50/10, 90/20) with automated focus/break cycles and gentle alarm sounds.
- **Stopwatch & Timer:** Full-featured precision stopwatch with lap tracking and countdown timer.
- **Layered Ambient Sound Mixer:** Combine multiple background sounds simultaneously (Rain, Fireplace, Waves, Birds, Cafe Noise, Train Journey, Winter Wind, Japanese Koto).
- **Curated Music Themes & Embed Player:** Built-in themed soundtracks (Cowboy Era, Cyber World, Dark Fantasy, Light Fantasy, Jazz & Romance, Rock n' Roll) + Spotify & YouTube embed loader.
- **To-Do List & Draggable Sticky Notes:** Quick task management and draggable aesthetic sticky notes saved locally.
- **Astrology & Birth Chart:** Real-time Zodiac, Element, Chinese Zodiac, Life Path number, and accurate Moon Phase calculation.
- **Dynamic Visuals & Video Backgrounds:** High-definition rotating themes, video backgrounds, or upload your own custom GIFs/images/videos.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Quick media control with Space (Play/Pause), F (Fullscreen), N/P (Next/Prev track), M (Mute).
- **Full Dual-Language Support:** Instant English and Turkish localization based on your system preference.

### Privacy & Permissions:
- `storage`: Used exclusively to save your to-do items, sticky notes, and preferences locally on your device.
- **Zero data collection:** Drunken Semaphore does not track, collect, or transmit any personal data.
