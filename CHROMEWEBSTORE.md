## Detailed Description (English)
Drunken Semaphore transforms your new tab into an aesthetic, focused, and relaxing handheld console sanctuary. Whether you are studying, coding, reading, or chilling, Drunken Semaphore provides everything you need in a unified, retro-futuristic glassmorphism workspace.

Key Features:
- Customizable Clock & World Clock: Minimalist digital time display, date, and multiple timezone trackers.
- Quick Access Hub: Fast-access workspace shortcuts for essential productivity tools, study platforms, and daily resources.
- Pomodoro Focus Timer: Pre-configured intervals (25/5, 50/10, 90/20) with automated focus/break cycles and gentle alarm sounds.
- Stopwatch & Timer: Precision stopwatch with lap tracking and countdown timer.
- Layered Ambient Sound Mixer: Combine multiple background sounds simultaneously (Rain, Fireplace, Ocean Waves, Forest Birds, Cafe Noise, Train Journey, Winter Wind, Classical Instruments).
- Curated Music Themes & Embed Player: Built-in themed soundtracks (Western, Cyberpunk, Dark Fantasy, Light Fantasy, Jazz, Rock n' Roll) along with an embed media player.
- To-Do List & Draggable Sticky Notes: Quick task management and draggable aesthetic sticky notes saved locally.
- Astrology & Lunar Calendar: Real-time Zodiac, Element, Chinese Zodiac, Life Path number, and accurate Moon Phase calculation.
- Dynamic Visuals & Video Backgrounds: High-definition rotating themes, video backgrounds, or upload your own custom animations and wallpapers.
- Keyboard Shortcuts: Quick media control with Space (Play/Pause), F (Fullscreen), N/P (Next/Prev track), M (Mute).
- Full Dual-Language Support: Instant English and Turkish localization based on your system preference.

Privacy & Permissions:
- storage: Used exclusively to save your to-do items, sticky notes, and preferences locally on your device.
- Zero data collection: Drunken Semaphore does not track, collect, or transmit any personal data.
